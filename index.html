A thoughtful way to give

Small stamps.
Big-hearted impact.

Collectible stamps can be of interest to collectors, and donated collections may be sold or otherwise used to raise funds for charitable work. Instead of letting yours gather dust, pass them along to an organization that can put them to good use.

The value and use of donated stamps varies. Please check with your chosen charity before sending a collection.

Three simple steps

Make room for good.

Getting your stamps ready to donate is easy. A little care helps them arrive in the best possible condition.

01 / Gather

Find your collection

Look through albums, drawers and old correspondence. Both used and unused stamps may be welcome—ask your chosen charity what it accepts.

02 / Protect

Pack them with care

Keep stamps dry and secure. If they're on envelopes, check whether the charity wants them left on paper or trimmed first.

03 / Pass it on

Choose a charity

Contact a charity or stamp-collecting fundraiser directly to confirm its mailing address, accepted stamps and any packing instructions.

Ready to give your stamps a new home?

Find a charity that accepts stamp donations, then check its guidelines before you mail your collection.

This page is an independent guide and does not collect or receive stamp donations.
